What is BHA?Qu'est-ce que la BHA?

Broken Hearts Association is the heart division of AltiMentum Global. A free community movement built for people carrying the invisible weight of life's most painful moments, grief, loss, trauma, broken relationships, professional collapse, displacement, exile, identity crisis, loneliness. People who have been through something that broke them and who need, above all, to be heard. BHA is not therapy. Not a religious institution. Not charity. It is a human community, with structured peer support, free hope-building resources, and a clear pathway from pain toward reconstruction and purpose.L'Association des Cœurs Brisés est la division du cœur d'AltiMentum Global. Un mouvement communautaire gratuit pour les personnes portant le poids invisible des moments les plus douloureux de la vie, deuil, perte, traumatisme, relations brisées, effondrement professionnel, déplacement, exil, crise d'identité, solitude.

Why Daniel Built BHAPourquoi Daniel a Construit la BHA

BHA was born from Daniel Mwendanga's own experience of knowing what it means to carry pain in silence, and his conviction that no person should walk that road alone. Having worked across Africa's most conflict-affected regions and having coached people through the deepest personal crises, Daniel built BHA as the most personal expression of AltiMentum Global's mission: to serve the whole person, not just their professional needs. The heart matters. The grief matters. You matter.La BHA est née de l'expérience personnelle de Daniel Mwendanga de savoir ce que signifie porter la douleur en silence, et de sa conviction qu'aucune personne ne devrait marcher seule sur ce chemin.

Why Standard Checks Fail in AfricaPourquoi les Vérifications Standard Échouent

International organizations entering Africa routinely rely on digital databases, tools designed for Western environments. Africa is different. Records are incomplete. Digital footprints are thin. But the truth is not absent. It lives in the community.Les organisations internationales entrant en Afrique s'appuient sur des bases de données numériques, des outils conçus pour les environnements occidentaux. L'Afrique est différente. Les archives sont incomplètes. Mais la vérité vit dans la communauté.
